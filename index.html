Medical records are where demand letters slow down.

A typical injury case arrives as hundreds of pages from a handful of providers: faxed visit notes, handwritten intake forms, imaging reports and itemized bills, out of order and full of duplicates. Someone has to read all of it to build the timeline, and every missed gap or prior complaint is one the adjuster will find first. Casewright does the reading and the first draft, so your people spend their time checking and arguing the case.

How it works

  1. 1

    Upload the records

    Drag in the PDFs for a matter, scanned, faxed or electronic. They go straight into your firm's private, encrypted storage.

  2. 2

    Casewright reads every page

    It sorts the pages, pulls out each visit, merges duplicates across providers and totals billed amounts.

  3. 3

    Your team reviews

    Gaps in treatment and pre-existing conditions are flagged, low-confidence fields are highlighted, and every row opens its source page.

  4. 4

    Export to the demand

    Download the reviewed chronology as Word or Excel, ready to drop into the demand letter or share with co-counsel.

Chronology

The timeline, built and cited

Security posture

Built for privileged records and protected health information

Casewright was designed by an engineer who has spent 14 years building software for the financial industry, where audits, encryption and access control are routine. These are the commitments behind every firm's environment.

US-only data residency

Records, backups and AI processing stay in United States cloud regions. Policies in the cloud account block any resource outside the US.

Your own isolated environment

Each firm runs in a separate cloud account with its own database, storage and encryption keys. Your files never share a database with another firm's.

Encrypted everywhere

TLS in transit and encryption at rest with keys dedicated to your firm, rotated automatically.

No training on your data

The AI service we use does not store your prompts or use them to train models. Document text is never written to application logs.

Human review, every time

AI drafts; your people decide. A chronology can't be exported until every row is reviewed, and the reviewer's name and time are recorded.

A full audit trail

Who viewed, edited, exported or asked what, and when. Audit records are kept for six years.

No standing access

Your team signs in with your own Microsoft 365 accounts and multi-factor authentication. We cannot open your documents day to day; emergency access needs your written approval and is logged.

A clean exit

If you leave, you get a full export, then your environment and its encryption keys are destroyed and you receive a certificate of destruction.

Designed with ABA Formal Opinion 512 on lawyers' use of generative AI in mind. Each firm receives a one-page vendor summary for its files and its malpractice carrier.

Questions attorneys ask

Where is our data stored?+

In your firm's own cloud account in the United States. Documents, the database and backups stay in US regions, and the AI models we use run only in US regions. Nothing is stored or processed outside the country.

Is the AI trained on our clients' records?+

No. The AI service processes each request and does not store your prompts or use them to train models. Your records are used only to produce your chronologies and answers.

What if the AI gets something wrong?+

That is why every row cites its source page and why nothing exports until a person reviews it. Uncertain fields are highlighted so your reviewer knows where to look. Casewright drafts; your team stays responsible for the work product, as with any paralegal draft.

Who at Casewright can see our documents?+

No one, day to day. Our operating access is set up so we can keep the system running without opening client files. If you ask us to troubleshoot something that requires it, access is time-limited, needs your written approval and is recorded in the audit log.

Is using this consistent with our ethical duties?+

ABA Formal Opinion 512 asks lawyers to understand how an AI tool handles confidential information and to review its output. We give you a plain-English vendor summary covering where data lives, who can access it and how output is reviewed. Whether a tool fits your obligations is your firm's judgment; we are not a law firm and don't give legal advice.

Do we have to change our case management software?+

No. Casewright works alongside whatever you use today. Chronologies export to Word and Excel, and integrations with practice management systems are on the roadmap, shaped by our pilot firms.

Will you sign a confidentiality agreement or BAA?+

Yes. Every firm gets a written confidentiality agreement, and a business associate agreement where HIPAA applies to your practice. You own your data; we hold it on your behalf.

What happens if we cancel?+

You receive a complete export of your documents and chronologies. Then we delete your environment and destroy its encryption keys, so any remaining backups become unreadable, and we send you a certificate of destruction.

Who is behind Casewright?+

Casewright is built by a software engineering leader with 14 years of experience building secure systems in financial technology. We serve firms across the United States, and you get a direct line to the person who built it, not a ticket queue.
